01 / ABOUT ME

ABOUT BI4BHQ

A RADIO AMATEUR FROM SHANGHAI, CHINA

Welcome to my personal amateur radio station. I am BI4BHQ, based in Jing'an District, Shanghai, China.

Amateur radio is more than communication. It is the fascination of sending electromagnetic waves into the unknown, reaching someone far away without relying on the internet or cellular networks.

Different places, different voices, one shared frequency. See you on the air!
